# Falloutization: Plants

## Changes

- Vanilla plants that were not removed unlocked by FCP Basic Agriculture research project (FCP Tools)
- Nutrifungus renamed to Cave Fungus, unlocked by FCP Mushroom Farming research project (FCP Tools)
- Made Vanilla Expanded planter boxes require FCP Advanced Agriculture research project (FCP Tools)
- Made FCP Advanced Agriculture (FCP Tools) ap rerequisite for Hydroponics, Scarecrow and Sprinkler (Vanilla Farming Expanded) research projects
